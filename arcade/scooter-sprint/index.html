HOW.TXT

How to play

  • The word at the top is on the next stoplight. Type it, letter by letter.
  • Finish the word, and the light turns green. Type fast, and Penny never has to stop.
  • Hit a wrong key? It blinks red. Just try the right one. Nothing is lost.
  • The little keyboard shows the next key, colored by which finger to use.
HANDS.TXT

Where your hands go

Rest your pointer fingers on F and J. Feel the little bumps? That's home.

Try not to look down. Peek at the keyboard on the screen instead.
