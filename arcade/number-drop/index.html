★ Orbi's Number Drop ★

Orbi needs fuel!

Catch falling numbers that add up to exactly the number at the top. Hit it right on, and a fuel cell lights up. Five cells, and Orbi blasts off.

Move with ← →, or drag Orbi with your finger or mouse.

HOW.TXT

How to play

  • The big number at the top is what Orbi needs, like Make 10.
  • Catch numbers that add up to it: 4, then 6. Or 3, 3 and 4.
  • Go over, and Orbi spits them out to start that number again. No harm done.
  • Missed numbers just fall in the grass. There's no losing.
CONTROLS.TXT

Controls

← → or A D to move.

On a tablet or phone, drag your finger along the bottom, and Orbi follows.

P or Esc to pause.
